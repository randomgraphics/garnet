# Assignment: CNC image transfers and upload staging arena
---
## Goals
Expose six buffer/image transfer methods on both CNC interfaces. Uploads copy CPU data into an internal staging arena; image copies transfer between textures.
---
## Design
CPU Region layouts use byte offsets, byte pitches between pixel-block rows, and byte pitches between depth slices. Offsets/extents in images remain texels, with compressed-block alignment and mip-edge exceptions.
Use private CNC-only mapped upload storage, repacking rows directly into aligned suballocations. Mappings move into payloads and close at submission; operation records retain backing buffers until GPU completion.
Removed public staged-texture loaders and buffer-to-image structures. CPU-image uploads cover all faces, mips, and depth slices. Image copies support explicit regions between distinct matching textures.
Synchronous texture setContent/readback and buffer setContent use tracked CNC transfers. Depth sampled descriptors and ready-state tracking share one format-appropriate read-only layout. Fixed volume creation preserving 3D extent and the cube-test face count.
