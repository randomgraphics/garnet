### Finding and bounded next experiment

The highest-confidence actionable discrepancy is face ordering: Khronos shader layers 2/3 are -Y/+Y, while Garnet DDS faces 2/3 are +Y/-Y. A secondary confirmed discrepancy is cube-edge source sampling: hardware seamless linear sampling versus Garnet's nearest-texel neighbor remap. The axis-sign comparison is suggestive but not conclusive without accounting for Vulkan viewport Y orientation and the shader's explicit Y inversion. These findings are source-level; I did not alter or regenerate any asset, and did not infer pixel equality from assignment 13's internal scalar test.
