# Assignment: GPU Resource Binding — Take 2

---

## Goal

**Comment out GpuResourceGroup entirely.** Fall back to a simpler model:

- Use a **3-D GpuResourceTable** to pass shader resources to shaders: `resources[setIndex][bindingIndex][arrayIndex]` = `GpuResourceView`.
- Rely on **rapid_vulkan's Drawable and DrawPack** to manage layout and descriptors behind the scene (no manual descriptor set allocation or vkCmdBindDescriptorSets from our side for resource groups).

So: actions and effect builders fill the table; the Vulkan backend converts table entries into `drawable.b()` / `drawable.t()` / `drawable.s()` calls; Drawable compiles to a DrawPack that owns descriptor sets and binding. We do not maintain GpuResourceGroup or its Vulkan descriptor-set implementation for now.

- DrawPack: layout and descriptors are compiled from the pipeline + bound arguments; CommandBuffer::render(drawPack) records the draw.
