# Assignment: Generic E2 visual domain and composed visual moments

---
## Goals
Keep the visual domain public interface independent of rendered content. Represent
world rendering, overlays, and environment lighting/background as visual moments.
Update active documentation and verify composition and rendering regressions.

---
## Design
World::snapshot() returns an opaque VisualTableau. The tableau public interface
has no storage, hierarchy, enumeration, traversal, or dependency API; applications
can add visual moments. Its current insertion-ordered storage lives in E2's private
implementation. Regular moments render in insertion order, then environments,
then overlays from larger logical Z to smaller Z. Environment ties and equal-Z
overlay ties have unspecified order. VisualMoment is a separate abstract item with a pure virtual
record(RenderContext&) operation. The borrowed context derives from RuntimeType
and exposes raster/upload services plus prepared FX2 shader bindings through ssc().
Custom moments receive the first scene's constants or defaults when no scene exists;
scene resource caches and scheduling stay private.
VisualOverlay and VisualEnvironment remain special moments declared after the
domain. Multiple environments render; scene lighting selects one environment with
unspecified selection when several exist. Each scene has separate camera UBOs.
The new public-interface skill requires actual cross-module contracts in public
headers and prefers pure virtual behavioral interfaces. FX2 residency, importer
format classification, and missing-attribute bookkeeping move to private files.
