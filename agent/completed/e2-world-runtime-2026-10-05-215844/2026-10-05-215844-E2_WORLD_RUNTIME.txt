# Assignment: E2 data-driven World runtime and falling-body validation

---
## Goals
Implement the approved Prime/Slate/Law runtime while preserving Form and Facet as
first-class application concepts. Demonstrate multiple falling bodies, persistent
contacts, lifetime-driven creation/deletion, and independent presentation.

Overview: the replacement object-oriented MVP is implemented and verified on
wip/e2/world-runtime, based on 51bdab91c. Form, Law, Intent and Event use Being; Facet and FacetValue use
RuntimeType + RefCounter without instance IDs or names; concrete Facets expose nested Values. Prime stores
immutable polymorphic versions and communicates only through base contracts.
Hierarchy and required-Facet validation are included. The work remains active
awaiting user review/sign-off. The user authorized committing and pushing this checkpoint.

---
## Design
The canonical design is src/core/e2/README.md. Every Law reads the same immutable
Prime; Slate is write-only. Each facet type has one writer Law. Initialization and
registered structural cleanup are restricted exceptions. Ordered Laws execute once
per tick; internally they may use substeps or parallel work. Pending intents are
transient and may be lost on failure. Failure discards candidate state/events and
halts advancement without automatic retry. Events become visible after publication.

DynamicsLaw precedes LifetimeLaw. It solves multiple bodies in a local workspace,
publishes symmetric final contact sets, and counts contact beginnings. LifetimeLaw
receives same-tick deletion intents for bodies below a configured Y threshold and
spawns at most 100 bodies/second, with at most 1000 generated bodies. No spawn credit
accumulates at capacity. Spawn clock/RNG are Prime state. Initial horizontal speed
is uniformly 0..0.5 m/s with uniform heading; initial vertical speed is zero.
Deleting a form discards its value writes and cleans contact references during
candidate preparation. New forms begin dynamics on the following tick.

Transform/Motion/Body remain separate MVP facets; acceleration fields are deferred.
Motion presence selects integration only for this demo, not as a universal physics
classification. The simple solver is not a general-purpose physics replacement.
Presentation reads retained snapshots on its own thread and uses public FX2 kernels.

Current hierarchy requirement (supersedes earlier Phase 4.2): TransformFacet values
store parent links and local poses in Prime; Form has no hierarchy or subtree deletion.
World poses are derived by composing the spatial ancestor chain. Facets declare required Facets using existing

initialization; no implicit construction. Adding/removing both together is valid.

provide compatible Laws; existing Laws need not understand or automatically
claim derived Facets. Polymorphic storage alone does not imply solver support.
Prime communicates exclusively through base Facet/FacetValue virtual contracts
and inherited Being RTTI. Subclass overrides customize those contracts; Prime
never branches on a concrete domain type or reads domain-specific fields.
