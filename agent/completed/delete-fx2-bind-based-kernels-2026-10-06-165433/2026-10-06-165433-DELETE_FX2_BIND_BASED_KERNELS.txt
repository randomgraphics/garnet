# Assignment: Delete FX2 Bind-Based Kernels

---

## Goals

Entirely remove all 9 bind-based FX2 kernels (classes deriving from `GN::fx2::Kernel`),
their public headers, core implementations, associated GLSL shaders, unit tests, and
legacy samples. Keep bind-based gpu2 features and `ImGuiBackend` intact.

---

## Design

### Decoupling `fiz` Sample Helpers
`src/sample/fiz/render-helpers.h` currently calls `LitKernelInputs::createBox` and
`createSphere` from `lit-kernels.h`. Make `boxGeometry` and `sphereGeometry` self-contained
in `render-helpers.h` so that `fiz` does not depend on `lit-kernels.h`.

### Kernel Headers to Remove
- `src/inc/garnet/fx2/kernel.h`
- `src/inc/garnet/fx2/lit-kernels.h`
- `src/inc/garnet/fx2/unlit-kernel.h`
- `src/inc/garnet/fx2/skybox-kernel.h`
- `src/inc/garnet/fx2/image-kernels.h`
- Remove includes from `src/inc/garnet/GNfx2.h`.

### Core Sources and Shaders to Remove
- `src/core/fx2/lit-kernels.cpp`
- `src/core/fx2/lit-kernel-geometry.cpp`
- `src/core/fx2/raster-kernels.cpp`
- `src/core/fx2/image-kernels.cpp`
- Associated vk-shaders in `src/core/fx2/vk-shaders/` (model, cel-model, cel-outline, image blur/mipmap, unlit, skybox).
- Update `src/core/fx2/CMakeLists.txt`.

### Tests and Samples to Remove
- `src/sample/fx2/simple-cel-shading.cpp`
- `src/sample/fx2/image-effects.cpp`
- Update `src/sample/fx2/CMakeLists.txt`.
- `src/core/fx2/test/kernel-test.cpp`
- `src/core/fx2/test/image-kernel-test.cpp`
- `src/core/fx2/test/lit-kernel-test.cpp`
- Update `src/core/fx2/test/internal-test.cmake`.
